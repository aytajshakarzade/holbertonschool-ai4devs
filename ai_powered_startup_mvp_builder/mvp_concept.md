# MVP Concept – FocusFlow

## Problem Statement
Managing daily tasks and staying productive can be challenging, especially when people have multiple responsibilities. FocusFlow helps users organize their work, manage priorities, and save time with AI-powered assistance.

## Target Users
- Students
- Freelancers
- Professionals
- Small teams

## Core Features
1. **Task Management:** Create, edit, delete, and organize tasks.
2. **AI Task Suggestions:** Get suggestions on how to organize and prioritize daily work.
3. **Progress Tracking:** Track completed and pending tasks.
4. **Smart Scheduling:** Organize tasks based on deadlines and priorities.
5. **Dashboard:** View tasks and productivity progress in one place.

## Constraints
- The MVP focuses on essential productivity features.
- AI suggestions may not always be accurate.
- No advanced team collaboration features in the initial version.
- Limited third-party integrations.
- The first version focuses on a simple and easy-to-use interface.
